# Proposal: TALENT / AGENCY Website

## Concept

TALENT / AGENCY will be a highly visual, chromeless website for a modern talent of represented online artists.

The site should not feel like a social network, creator marketplace, casting database, or SaaS product. It should feel like an agency with taste: selective, minimal, image-led, and built around the people it represents.

The main purpose is simple:

**Show the talent. Feature the artists. Provide links. Route serious inquiries through the agency.**

## Brand Direction

The name TALENT / AGENCY works because it is direct, minimal, and almost anti-brand. It turns a generic phrase into a visual identity.

The brand should be presented bluntly and confidently:

**TALENT**
**AGENCY**

or

**TALENT / AGENCY**

The site should use the name as a design object: large type, sharp layouts, simple language, and strong visual contrast. The brand should not over-explain itself. It should feel like a cool office, a talent wall, a gallery, and an editorial platform at the same time.

Suggested positioning line:

**A visual talent of represented online artists.**

## Design Direction

The site should be very visual and nearly chromeless. The interface should get out of the way so the artists become the experience.

Design priorities:

* large artist imagery
* minimal navigation
* oversized typography
* dark editorial backgrounds
* soft motion
* clean profile layouts
* full-screen or near full-screen artist features
* simple links instead of overbuilt tools
* strong mobile browsing
* no cluttered cards
* no dashboard feeling
* no fake social media behavior

The visual reference should be closer to a fashion label, record label, gallery, film studio, or art magazine than a traditional talent agency website.

The homepage should immediately feel curated. It should open with a strong artist feature, rotating talent visuals, bold names, and a direct path into the talent.

## Core Experience

The site should have a very simple flow:

1. Visitor lands on a striking visual homepage.
2. Visitor enters the talent.
3. Visitor browses represented artists.
4. Visitor opens an artist page.
5. Visitor clicks external links or sends an inquiry through the agency.

The site does not need user accounts, public posting, direct messaging, media kit downloads, or creator-platform features.

This is not another place for artists to “build a profile.”
This is the official talent for people the agency represents.

## Talent Page

The talent page is the heart of the website.

It should be image-first, fast, and elegant. Artists should appear in a visual layout that feels curated rather than database-driven.

Possible talent treatments:

* full-screen visual grid
* large portrait tiles
* alternating image sizes
* hover or tap name reveals
* quick category tags
* simple search
* minimal filters
* featured artist rows
* new additions
* agency picks
* currently active / booking highlights

Filtering should exist only as a convenience. The main feeling should still be browsing a curated talent, not operating software.

## Artist Pages

Each artist page should be focused, beautiful, and link-forward.

An artist page should include:

* artist name
* hero image or featured visual
* short agency-written bio
* selected visuals
* selected work links
* social links
* video/music/platform links where relevant
* credits or notable highlights
* inquiry button
* agency contact path

The page should not try to replace the artist’s full internet presence. It should point outward clearly and tastefully.

The artist page should answer:

**Who is this?**
**Why do they matter?**
**Where can I see more?**
**How do I inquire?**

## Convenience Features

The site should provide practical conveniences without becoming bloated.

Recommended features:

* clean artist search
* simple talent filters
* external link blocks
* per-artist inquiry button
* shareable artist URLs
* featured talent controls
* admin-editable artist profiles
* homepage featured artist selection
* highlight/news posts for recent work
* optional private agency-created shortlists

The key rule: every feature should support representation, discovery, or inquiry.

No social feed.
No public profiles.
No follower mechanics.
No creator dashboard energy on the public site.

## Main Pages

**Homepage**
A bold visual introduction to TALENT / AGENCY, featuring the talent, selected artists, and a simple call to view the talent.

**Talent**
The central browsing experience for represented artists.

**Artist Profile**
A minimal, visual profile with selected work, links, short copy, and agency inquiry action.

**Highlights**
A curated section for recent work, bookings, releases, appearances, press, and agency updates.

**About**
A short agency statement focused on representation, taste, online culture, and the talent.

**Contact / Inquiry**
A direct inquiry form for bookings, collaborations, partnerships, press, and general contact.

## Homepage Copy Direction

Possible hero copy:

**TALENT / AGENCY**
**Representing artists online.**

Supporting line:

**A curated talent of creators, performers, and public personalities.**

Primary actions:

**View Talent**
**Inquire**

Alternative copy:

**A visual talent of represented online artists.**
**Built for discovery, links, and serious inquiries.**

## Visual Tone

The site should feel:

* cool
* sparse
* visual
* premium
* internet-native
* editorial
* confident
* selective
* artist-centered
* modern without being trendy

The brand should be simple enough to grow. It can represent online creators now, while still leaving room for actors, musicians, comedians, filmmakers, writers, hosts, and other public-facing artists later.

## Final Goal

The final website will give TALENT / AGENCY a sharp, modern presence built around its talent.

It will make the agency feel real, curated, and visually strong. It will make represented artists look important. It will give visitors a simple way to explore, follow links, and inquire.

The site should say, without overexplaining:

**These are our artists.
This is our talent.
Contact us if you want to work with them.**
